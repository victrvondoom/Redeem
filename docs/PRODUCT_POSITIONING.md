# Product positioning
**REDEEM is an execution-and-verification layer, not a voice assistant.** A person describes a problem by voice; REDEEM plans the cross-service work, asks for approval bound to the exact target and amount, executes through connectors, then independently reads the result back and gives evidence.

- **Category:** voice-first, multi-domain action platform with verified outcomes.
- **One-liner:** "Speak your need. We take the lead — and show you what actually happened."
- **For whom (first):** an individual dealing with a travel disruption that touches an airline, a hotel, their calendar and family. **Second:** a small Indian retailer whose phone calls should become verified reservations.
- **Not claiming:** customers, revenue, retention, live-call performance, or that any listed language works well in live voice. None is evidenced yet.
- **Honest status line (use verbatim):** "Every outcome shown today runs against labelled local fixtures; live Bolna calling is implemented and contract-tested but not yet exercised."
